**Strategic Annual Plan: Logistics Costs Reduction and Business Targets Alignment**

**Executive Summary:**
The strategic annual plan aims to address the identified gaps in logistics costs across Belgium, Sweden, and Portugal. By implementing the recommended actions, we will reduce commercial costs, aligning with internal business goals and focusing on the identified gaps.

**Recommended Actions and Implementation Plan:**

**Belgium:**

1. **Action 1: Reduce commercial costs by 44.7% uniformly across all months**
	* Monthly reduction: €252.2k (€3,026.6k / 12 months)
	* New monthly target: €561.1k (€6,773.4k / 12 months - €252.2k)
	* Implementation timeline: Month 1-12
	* Responsible team: Logistics and Procurement
	* Key performance indicators (KPIs):
		+ Monthly commercial costs reduction
		+ Yearly commercial costs reduction (target: €3,026.6k)
2. **Monitoring and Evaluation:**
	* Regular review of monthly commercial costs
	* Quarterly review of progress toward the yearly target
	* Adjustment of strategies as needed

**Sweden:**

1. **Action 1: Reduce commercial costs by 31.1% uniformly across all months**
	* Monthly reduction: €236.9k (€2,844.8k / 12 months)
	* New monthly target: €763.0k (€9,155.2k / 12 months - €236.9k)
	* Implementation timeline: Month 1-12
	* Responsible team: Logistics and Procurement
	* KPIs:
		+ Monthly commercial costs reduction
		+ Yearly commercial costs reduction (target: €2,844.8k)
2. **Monitoring and Evaluation:**
	* Regular review of monthly commercial costs
	* Quarterly review of progress toward the yearly target
	* Adjustment of strategies as needed

**Portugal:**

* No action required, as there is an overperformance.
* Continue to monitor and evaluate monthly commercial costs to ensure sustained performance.

**Prioritization and Resource Allocation:**

* High priority: Implementation of cost reduction actions in Belgium and Sweden
* Medium priority: Monitoring and evaluation of progress in Belgium and Sweden
* Low priority: Monitoring of sustained performance in Portugal

**Budget Allocation:**

* Belgium: €3,026.6k (yearly reduction target)
* Sweden: €2,844.8k (yearly reduction target)
* Portugal: No additional budget required

**Timeline:**

* Month 1-3: Implementation of cost reduction actions in Belgium and Sweden
* Month 4-6: Review of progress and adjustment of strategies as needed
* Month 7-12: Continued monitoring and evaluation of progress

By following this strategic annual plan, we will address the identified gaps in logistics costs, align with internal business goals, and focus on the identified gaps. Regular monitoring and evaluation will ensure that we are on track to meet our targets and make adjustments as needed.
