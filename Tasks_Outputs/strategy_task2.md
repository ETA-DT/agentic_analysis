**Strategic Annual Plan: Reducing Logistics Costs in Finland and Portugal**

**Executive Summary:**
This strategic annual plan outlines a comprehensive approach to address the identified gaps in logistics costs in Finland and Portugal. By implementing the recommended actions, we aim to achieve a reduction of 8.78% and 10.87% in logistics costs in Finland and Portugal, respectively, uniformly across all 12 months.

**Action Plan:**

**Quarter 1 (January-March):**

1. **Finland:**
	* Implement a cost-saving initiative to reduce monthly logistics costs by 8.78% (€68.18k) (January-February).
	* Review and renegotiate contracts with logistics providers to achieve a minimum of 8.78% reduction in monthly costs (February-March).
	* Optimize logistics operations to reduce waste and inefficiencies, targeting a monthly cost reduction of 8.78% (March).
2. **Portugal:**
	* Introduce a cost-reduction program to decrease monthly logistics costs by 10.87% (€81.25k) (January-February).
	* Analyze and adjust logistics routes to minimize fuel consumption and lower emissions, aiming for a 10.87% reduction in monthly costs (February-March).
	* Streamline logistics processes to eliminate unnecessary expenses and achieve a monthly cost reduction of 10.87% (March).

**Quarter 2 (April-June):**

1. **Finland:**
	* Monitor and evaluate the effectiveness of the cost-saving initiative implemented in Quarter 1 (April-May).
	* Identify areas for further improvement and implement additional cost-saving measures (May-June).
	* Continue to optimize logistics operations to maintain the 8.78% monthly cost reduction (June).
2. **Portugal:**
	* Assess the impact of the cost-reduction program introduced in Quarter 1 (April-May).
	* Implement adjustments to logistics routes and processes to maintain the 10.87% monthly cost reduction (May-June).
	* Continue to streamline logistics processes to eliminate unnecessary expenses (June).

**Quarter 3 (July-September):**

1. **Finland:**
	* Review and renegotiate contracts with logistics providers to ensure the 8.78% reduction in monthly costs is maintained (July-August).
	* Implement additional cost-saving measures to further reduce logistics costs (August-September).
	* Continue to optimize logistics operations to maintain the 8.78% monthly cost reduction (September).
2. **Portugal:**
	* Analyze and adjust logistics routes to minimize fuel consumption and lower emissions, aiming for a 10.87% reduction in monthly costs (July-August).
	* Streamline logistics processes to eliminate unnecessary expenses and achieve a monthly cost reduction of 10.87% (August-September).
	* Continue to monitor and evaluate the effectiveness of the cost-reduction program (September).

**Quarter 4 (October-December):**

1. **Finland:**
	* Review and evaluate the effectiveness of the cost-saving initiative implemented throughout the year (October-November).
	* Identify areas for further improvement and implement additional cost-saving measures (November-December).
	* Continue to optimize logistics operations to maintain the 8.78% monthly cost reduction (December).
2. **Portugal:**
	* Assess the impact of the cost-reduction program introduced throughout the year (October-November).
	* Implement adjustments to logistics routes and processes to maintain the 10.87% monthly cost reduction (November-December).
	* Continue to streamline logistics processes to eliminate unnecessary expenses (December).

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**

1. Reduction in logistics costs in Finland: 8.78% (€68.18k) per month.
2. Reduction in logistics costs in Portugal: 10.87% (€81.25k) per month.
3. Implementation of cost-saving initiatives and renegotiation of contracts with logistics providers.
4. Optimization of logistics operations to reduce waste and inefficiencies.
5. Streamlining of logistics processes to eliminate unnecessary expenses.

**Budget Allocation:**

1. Finland: €68.18k per month (€818.16k per year).
2. Portugal: €81.25k per month (€975k per year).

**Timeline:**

* Quarter 1: January-March.
* Quarter 2: April-June.
* Quarter 3: July-September.
* Quarter 4: October-December.

**Responsible Parties:**

1. Logistics Manager: Finland.
2. Logistics Manager: Portugal.
3. Procurement Manager: Finland and Portugal.
4. Operations Manager: Finland and Portugal.

By following this strategic annual plan, we aim to achieve a reduction of 8.78% and 10.87% in logistics costs in Finland and Portugal, respectively, uniformly across all 12 months, addressing the identified gaps and improving overall logistics efficiency.
